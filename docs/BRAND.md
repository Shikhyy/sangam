# Sangam brand guide

One page, so the look and the voice stay the same as the product grows.

## The name

Sangam (संगम) means a meeting point, the place where rivers join. That is the whole idea: Hindi and English, two streams, one voice. Write it **Sangam**, with a capital S. In display type the Devanagari **संगम** can stand in for it.

## What it says

- **Line:** A voice agent that talks like you.
- **One sentence:** Sangam measures how you mix Hindi and English, then keeps an agent's replies in the same mix.
- **For builders:** A layer, not a model. It sits between the transcript and the reply, so it works with any speech stack and any language model.

## Voice and tone

- Plain and specific. Say what it does and show a number.
- Honest. Say what is planned and what is live. Never show a result we have not measured; placeholders are marked `[ value ]`.
- Warm, not cute. It is a tool for people who talk the way they talk.
- No jargon in the headline. "Code-switching" belongs in the docs, not the hero.
- Hinglish examples are real sentences people say, written in Roman script unless the point is Devanagari.

## The mark

A rounded olive tile. A warm strand and a pale strand curve in from the left and join into one white line. The tile is `#6e7152` to `#4b4d36`; the strands are `#ffa14a` and `#f4efe0`; the joined line is white. Use it at 24px or larger, with clear space of one strand width around it. Do not rotate it, recolour it, or put it on a busy background. The same mark is the site icon.

## Colour

Warm paper, olive, and two lane colours that always mean the same thing.

| Token   | Value                      | Use                      |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Paper   | `#f2efe4`                  | Page ground              |
| Ink     | `#26231e`                  | Text                     |
| Olive   | `#5b5d43`                  | Actions and the mark     |
| Hindi   | `#ff8a1f` (text `#a8460a`) | Anything that is Hindi   |
| English | `#3d2f7a`                  | Anything that is English |

Never use the Hindi and English colours for anything else, and never tell them apart by colour alone: pair them with a label or a lane.

## Type

- **Display:** Instrument Serif, with Tiro Devanagari for Hindi. Roman for statements, italic for the word that matters.
- **Interface:** Geist. **Numbers and labels in code:** Geist Mono.

## Motion

One idea: things flow and join. The painted sky moves with your mix, headlines blur into focus, sections rise as you reach them. All motion stops for people who ask their device to reduce it.

## Naming the future product

Plan names follow the verbs a team does with it: **Try**, **Build**, **Scale**. They are planned, not sold, and carry no prices until we have set them.

## Do not

- Imply a customer, a number or a language that does not exist yet.
- Say "AI-powered" as a headline.
- Show a Hindi sentence in a font that cannot draw Devanagari.
